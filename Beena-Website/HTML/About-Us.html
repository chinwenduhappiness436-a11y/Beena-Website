Building Trust Through Quality Services

Beena Universal Links Limited began its journey in March 2006 as Beena Integrated Services. The company was established to support the Marine, Oil and Gas, and Non-Oil and Gas industries in Nigeria and beyond.

Through its range of services, the company focuses on providing unique and quality solutions that meet the needs of its clients.
